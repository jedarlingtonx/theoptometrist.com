Proudly independent

Independent for 36 years. Still owned by the two who started it.

Since 1990, Clare & Illingworth has served the communities of Knutsford and Wilmslow, owned exclusively by its original founders.

  • Independently owned. Not part of a national chain, corporate group or buying group.
  • Proudly local. Lasting relationships with generations of patients in Knutsford and Wilmslow.
  • Personal service. We take the time to get to know you and offer genuinely individual care.
  • Professional teams. Optometrists, dispensing opticians and practice teams at the heart of everything.
  • The freedom to do things our way. We choose the products, services and care we believe are right for you.

Behind the test

A closer look at your eye health.

Catching early warning signs makes treatment easier and more manageable. Our diagnostic equipment helps your optometrist see more.

Visual field machine

Maps how much you can see around the edges of your vision.

HRT

The Heidelberg Retina Tomograph, to assess the back of the eye.

Retinal camera

A clear picture of your retina, kept on record to compare over time.

Relaxed Vision Terminal

Takes a still image of you in your frame, so lenses are tailored like a great suit.

Eyewear

Measured by experts. Made to measure.

Accurate measurements come from experience and confidence, which is why we only employ fully qualified Dispensing Opticians.

A Dispensing Optician completes a three-year diploma, so the person choosing with you understands your prescription and what it means for your lenses.

As an independent practice, we choose from lens options across the UK market, and trust Zeiss and Nikon for a high-quality finish every time.
  1. We assess your prescriptionThen help you choose a suitable frame from the many in-store.
  2. We take your measurementsA few facial measurements, so you get the best vision through your new lenses.
  3. We tailor your lensesThe RVT sends a series of measurements to the lab, for bespoke lenses.
  4. Trusted partner labs make themYour frame goes to a partner lab we trust to make the perfect pair.

Contact lenses

Freedom from your frames.
